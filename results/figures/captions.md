# Figure captions (notes, not manuscript text)

- fig01_prisma_flow (png/svg). Counts from code/finalize_screening.py (data/release/screening_log.csv). Caution: single screener; "triage-negative" boxes were not checked by eye.
- fig02_coverage_lake_year.png. Lake (30 with most source-years) by year 2000-2026; cell = number of distinct sources (studies + KSPCB + CPCB + DMG 2011) whose stated period overlaps the year. Data: t02_coverage_lake_by_year_nsources.csv. Caution: official series cover whole years even when only some months were sampled; undated studies are absent.
- fig03_parameter_family_year.png. Parameter family by year, number of distinct sources. Caution: counts sources, not values; KSPCB metals appear only in some 2024-25 months.
- fig04_literature_by_year.png. Records retained at title/abstract screening and the subset extracted. Caution: extraction subset is limited by open full-text availability, not random.
- fig05_map_lakes_study_counts (png/svg/interactive html). Lake points from OpenStreetMap; size = candidate papers mentioning the lake name in title/abstract (among 332 retained); colour = studies extracted; grey = no KSPCB/CPCB series. Caution: name matching is approximate; about 40 percent of lake keys (80 of 202) have no coordinates; "Puttenahalli" is two lakes; a few matches are medium-low confidence (see data/release/lakes.csv coord_confidence). Interactive HTML loads OSM tiles from the viewer's browser.
- fig06_kspcb_class_by_month.png. Best CPCB designated-use class met per lake-month (A-E, below E), computed from DO, BOD, total coliform, pH, ammoniacal-N (used as a proxy for free ammonia), EC and boron where printed. Caution: KSPCB prints its own class; ours is recomputed. Free ammonia is not the same as total ammoniacal-N; BOD basis differs from the 5 d/20 C convention; DO values flagged BDL (0.3) are detection limits.
- fig07_cpcb_balanced_panel.png. Medians across the 41 stations present in every CPCB table 2016-2024 of annual maximum BOD, annual minimum DO and annual maximum faecal coliform. Caution: annual min/max are extremes and depend on how many months were sampled; the 2023 file covers a part-year in KSPCB's mirror.
- fig08_hebbal_cross_source.png. BOD and DO at Hebbal Lake from a 2013 thesis (8 stations x 12 months), KSPCB monthly 2023-25 and CPCB annual ranges 2016-24. Caution: CPCB 2023-24 ranges are derived from the KSPCB monthly data (not independent); station codes 3625/3627 pairing relies on the CPCB name column.
